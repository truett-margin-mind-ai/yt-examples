About The Role

We're seeking a Senior Reporting Analyst to own partner-facing analytics and reporting while building the processes for our data analytics function. You'll deliver actionable insights on license utilization, engagement, satisfaction, and performance metrics through both proactive reporting and a structured intake process.

This role requires strong systems thinking to understand how data flows across our ecosystem and how to synthesize information from multiple sources into cohesive narratives. You'll produce high-quality reports and dashboards while establishing frameworks for scalable analytics delivery, including leadership of offshore/BPO analyst resources. You should be comfortable leveraging AI and agentic workflows to accelerate analysis, identify patterns, and automate routine reporting.

What You’ll Do

Analytics Intake & Request Management

Serve as primary point of contact for analytics requests; triage, prioritize, and manage request queue
Document patterns to identify automation opportunities and leverage AI tools to accelerate scoping

Partner Analytics & Reporting

Design and deliver comprehensive reports on license utilization, engagement, satisfaction, and behavioral patterns
Analyze manager assessments (360-degree feedback, leadership development metrics) and coach feedback themes
Monitor support tickets and create actionable reports identifying systemic issues
Prepare templated Business Value Review (BVR) decks and execute custom ad-hoc analyses
Use AI-powered analytics to automatically surface anomalies and trends

Systems Thinking & Data Integration

Map data flows across analytics ecosystem (Looker, Vitally, Salesforce, support systems, survey platforms)
Understand metric relationships and synthesize disparate data sources into unified narratives
Translate complex, multi-system data relationships into clear visualizations
Identify opportunities for single sources of truth and eliminate conflicting data definitions

AI & Agentic Analytics Integration

Leverage AI tools to accelerate workflows from exploration to insight generation
Design agentic workflows where AI autonomously generates reports and surfaces insights
Establish validation processes for AI-generated analyses and partner with AI Engineer on enhancements
Train offshore analysts on effective AI tool usage and monitor outputs for quality

Team Structure & Offshore Analytics Enablement

Build frameworks for prioritizing requests and routing to appropriate resources (senior analyst, offshore team, AI-powered self-service, automated reporting)
Develop SOPs, templates, quality standards, and enablement programs for offshore/BPO analysts
Mentor offshore team and teach them to validate AI analyses and escalate appropriately

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Partner with Data & Insights team on new models, dashboards, self-service tools, and AI-powered solutions
Contribute to data governance initiatives and optimize analytics processes with Operations Program Manager
