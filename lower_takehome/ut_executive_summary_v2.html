5 Facebook deposits producing $705K in matched revenue. (2 additional deposits from LinkedIn post boosting are excluded.) Small sample, but dramatically more efficient per dollar than Google Ads. Most Meta campaigns were paused in 2025.
Cohort analysis confirms diminishing returns on Google Ads. Q1 2024 produced 9 deposits on $26K spend (57x ROAS); Q1 2025 produced 8 deposits on $118K (11x). Note: Late-2025 cohorts are still immature — 59% of H2 2025 paid search deals remain active.

Conquest: Strong Performance, Structural Fixes Needed
Conquest campaigns target competitor brand keywords (Midwest Automotive Designs, Becker, Grech RV, Camping World). At $25K spend: 9 deals, 4 deposits (44.4%), 0% DQ. People searching for competitors are already serious buyers. In H2 2025 alone, Conquest produced 4 deposits at $12K — a better return than $8K of PMax. Note: The paid media team has identified structural issues with the Conquest campaign setup that need to be addressed before any budget reallocation.
RV & Mobility Vans: Likely Poor Performers
RVs converts Google Ads clicks to real HubSpot contacts at 3–5x lower rates than other vehicle campaigns (8.9% vs 24–45%). Of the 32 RV contacts we can track, zero converted to deposits. Mobility has 150 GA "conversions" but zero identifiable HubSpot contacts. Combined $114K in spend with no evidence of producing sales.
Camper Vans Efficiency Degrading
Camper Vans cost/conversion rose from $104 in April 2025 to $431 in December 2025 — a 4x increase. Meanwhile, Sprinter Vans improved from $340–618 in early 2025 to $41–180 in H2 2025 after budget was scaled up.
Mercedes / Conversion Vans is the top performer across both Google Ads volume and HubSpot close rates. Its 30.4% deposit rate with zero DQs makes it the most reliable vehicle category. Sprinter Vans offers the best Google Ads efficiency ($126/conv) with solid HubSpot conversion. RVs and Mobility show poor performance at every level — highest CPCs, lowest conversion-to-contact rates, and zero trackable deposits.

Recommended Actions
Eight steps to improve lead quality and ad efficiency
1
Test reducing HIC spend by 25%. Redirect to broader Search campaigns. The cost premium is widening and HIC states convert at 3–6x lower rates than Ohio.
2
Invest in SEO. Organic Search converts at 21.9% — the highest digital channel — at $0 marginal ad cost. With Google Ads showing diminishing returns, SEO is the most capital-efficient growth lever.
3
Test incremental Meta Ads budget. ~37x Facebook ROAS at $19K is a strong signal. Run a controlled test at $5–10K/month before scaling Google Ads further.
4
Review Camper Vans budget. Cost/conversion rose 4x (Apr–Dec 2025). Consider pausing or reducing budget while Sprinter Vans and Mercedes campaigns scale more efficiently.
5
Reduce Mobility Vans and RV budgets by 50%; reallocate to Mercedes / Conversion Vans. These are the two most expensive campaigns (RVs $5.35 CPC, Mobility $3.85) with the lowest conversion-to-contact rates (9% and 0% vs 25–45% for other vehicles). Even tracked RV leads show zero deposits.
6
Filter junk leads before they enter the pipeline. ~35% of DQs are unreachable contacts, ~14% confirmed bots. Add phone validation and CAPTCHA/honeypot on forms to reduce wasted rep time.
7
Investigate Direct Traffic attribution. 1,001 deals at 42.7% DQ suggests attribution loss. Verify UTMs are preserved through the new form flow.
8
Add UTM campaign tags for all vehicle types. Enables campaign-level attribution in HubSpot. RVs and Mobility have the weakest tracking — fixing this will confirm whether the reduced budgets should be cut entirely or restored.
