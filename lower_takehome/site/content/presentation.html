Speed to contact is a direct revenue lever. Shifting dialing capacity from late attempts (11–20) to early contact on fresh leads improves both speed-to-contact and conversion probability. Day 0 contacts fund at 0.88% — by Day 5+, the funded rate drops to ~0%. Every dial reclaimed from a low-probability attempt and redirected to a same-day first contact is worth materially more in expected revenue.
Weekends are the biggest untapped window. Weekend contact rates are 3x higher than weekdays, but dialing volume is 10–15x lower. We're under-investing in the most productive calling window. Weekends still generate ~55% of weekday lead volume — those leads deserve comparable coverage.

Quality vs. Speed: Lowest Value Gets Fastest Response
Funded Rate by Loan Type
The lowest-value channel gets the fastest response. Digital Buy Partner (0.12% funded) averages 28.5 hours to first dial — the fastest of any channel — because 46% of its leads are auto-dialed by the speed-to-lead campaign. Meanwhile Direct/Organic (2.15% funded, 18x more valuable) averages 37.9 hours and only 31% are auto-dialed. Paid Spend (1.05% funded) leads are never called 26.4% of the time.
Speed-to-lead campaign insight: 10,518 leads (40.6% of dialed leads) are auto-dialed instantly via a "speed-to-lead" campaign. The auto-dialer lifts contact rates across all channels (+4pp overall), and shows the strongest funded-rate lift on high-value channels — Direct/Organic auto-dialed leads fund at 1.48% vs 1.16% manual. But the campaign disproportionately serves Digital Buy Partner (46% auto-dialed) over Direct/Organic (31%). Reweighting toward quality would amplify returns.
6
Three changes to turn more dials into funded loans
Reallocate effort from low-yield volume to high-yield precision
Highest ROI
1
Cap Pool A at 10 Attempts
Right now, agents continue dialing leads up to 20 times — even though nearly all funded loans happen in the first few attempts. Attempts 11–20 produce zero funded loans but consume 36% of total dials. By stopping after attempt 10, we reclaim ~103K dials per quarter and redirect that capacity toward fresh, high-probability leads where conversion probability is highest.
Est. Impact: +$120–170K/quarter (~28 additional funded loans)
2
Implement Channel-Aware Prioritization
All channels currently wait the same ~10-13 hours median despite an 18x gap in funded rate. Introducing quality-weighted priority — routing Direct/Organic and Paid Spend leads to the front of the queue — would direct speed to where it drives funded loans.
Est. Impact: +$75–100K/quarter (~15 additional funded loans)
3
Add Weekend Staffing
Weekend contact rates are 3x higher than weekdays, but dialing volume is 10–15x lower. We're under-investing in the most productive window. Adding 2–3 reps on Saturday and Sunday — when leads are still coming in at ~55% of weekday volume — would yield disproportionate contact and conversion gains.
Est. Impact: +$75–100K/quarter (~15 additional funded loans)
The big idea: The single highest-ROI change is capping Pool A at 10 attempts — this alone frees 36% of dial capacity with zero loss in funded loans. Pairing that with channel-aware prioritization and weekend staffing turns reclaimed capacity into revenue. Together, these three changes estimate an incremental ~$270–370K per quarter (~$1.1–1.5M/year) in funded loan revenue.
Estimates assume ~$6,000 blended revenue per funded loan (weighted by loan-type mix) and current baseline conversion rates. See appendix for detailed assumptions.
