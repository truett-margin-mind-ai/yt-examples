Initial Business Context
• Our Direct-to-Consumer Line of Business (DTC) handles consumers who are looking for help 
purchasing a home or refinancing an existing loan, or just looking to get some cash for a big 
purchase or to pay off some debt using a HELOC.
• DTC typically handles thousands of leads per month from multiple sources – some organic, some 
through Paid Marketing efforts such as Google Ads run by our growth team, and some that we 
purchase through our partner network.
• Typically, we will engage with these leads through a mixture of organic outreach such as Calendly 
scheduling; other times, we own the outreach with phone calls and SMS, with the goal of
contacting the consumer, understanding their needs, and getting them into our loan pipeline for 
our Loan Officers.
• The purpose of this exercise is to focus in on the call activity for these leads, not SMS or Calendly
schedules. Not every lead gets a phone call. Those with a NULL last contact attempt time have 
not been called, typically on purpose, as they do not meet business requirements for 
engagement.
• Once a consumer submits a lead form on our website, our system will automatically route that 
consumer to the next available Loan officer assistant or sales development representative or 
licensed loan officer to take the phone call. We will make 3 calls maximum on any given day 
within business hours to a consumer, and we will look to call more recent leads and more recent 
lead earlier attempt numbers vs. older leads and later attempt numbers (LIFO concept). 
• Once these consumers are connected with our contact center staff and are interested in 
mortgage help, they will be transferred to our Licensed Loan Officers. From here, they go through 
loan milestones such as file started, preapproval, loan locked, and ultimately funded loan.
• Milestone rates, and how these sources of leads are getting through our contact center and 
through our conversion funnel are extremely important to us – the more consumers we contact, 
turn into opportunities, into preapprovals and ultimately funded loans, the more consumers we 
can ultimately help and improve our overall process into the future.

Lead ID Unique identifier for the lead record. Each lead represents a consumer who 
submitted information to inquire about a mortgage product.
Lead Created Time Timestamp when the lead record was created in the system (EST). Represents the 
moment the lead entered the funnel.
Lead Created Date Date Part of the Created Timestamp for easier filtering/pivoting, etc.
Last Contact Attempt 
Time

actually called. Lead data set will give you all leads. We typically only call leads that 
have met our business requirements for engagement.
UTM Source Marketing source captured from the UTM parameters at the time of lead creation 
(e.g., Google, Facebook, Zillow). Identifies the origin of traffic.
UTM Medium Marketing medium captured from the UTM parameters (e.g., CPC, Paid Social,

universe of UTM and channel detail segmentation to see where the lead came from

Call Activity Data Set:
Field Definition
Activity ID Unique identifier for the call activity record. Each row represents a single call event 
logged in the activity dataset. Surrogate Primary Key.
Lead ID Unique identifier of the lead associated with this call activity. Used to join to dim_lead
for lead-level attributes.
Lead Created Time Timestamp when the lead record was first created in the system (EST). Represents 
the exact time the lead entered the funnel.
Lead Created Date Date portion of the lead creation timestamp, used for grouping or filtering by calendar 
date.
Activity Time Timestamp when the call activity occurred (EST). Represents the precise time the call 
attempt was made or received.
Activity Date Date portion of the activity timestamp. Used for daily aggregations and trending.
First Contact Time Timestamp of the first successful contact attempt with the lead (EST). Null if the lead 
has not yet been reached.
Contacted? Boolean flag indicating whether the call resulted in a successful contact with the lead 
(e.g., lead answered duration of call > 29s).
Direction Indicates whether the call was Inbound (lead called us) or Outbound (we called the 
lead).
Incoming Lead? Boolean flag indicating if the call originated from a newly created inbound lead (e.g., 
webform or phone inquiry).
Is Live Transfer? Boolean flag indicating that the contact center team member transferred this 
consumer to a Loan Officer. The Loan Officer then typically talks to consumer and 
starts a file and process for underwriting a loan.
Outcome Result or disposition of the call activity (e.g., Contacted, No Answer, Voicemail, 
Disconnected). Reflects how the call ended.
Team Member ID Unique identifier for the team member (agent or loan officer) who handled the call.
Team Member Name Full name of the team member associated with the call activity.
Team Member Role The functional role or position of the team member (e.g., Loan Officer, ISA, Agent).
Pool A call Attempt 
Number
Sequential count of call attempts made to the same lead. The first attempt = 1, 
increasing with each additional attempt for Pool A Calls. Pool A represents dials 1- 20
Is Latest Pool A Dial 
Attempt
Boolean flag to indicate if the call attempt number is the latest attempt
Is Pool A (Active SDR)? Boolean flag to indicate if the call cadence is still in Pool A (dials 1 – 20). Null either 
indicates call attempts have exceeded 20 or call cadence has finished for other 
reasons. Not all calls are Pool A, excludes inbound, click to dial, inbound etc.
Call Attempt Number Sequential count of call attempts made to the same lead. The first attempt = 1, 
increasing with each additional attempt.
Is Latest Dial Attempt? Boolean Flag to indicate if current dial attempt is the latest.
Calls Count of call activities logged for the lead or team member within the selected 
period.
Contacts Count of call activities that resulted in successful contact (answered calls) within the 
selected period.
Transfers Sent Count of call activities where the team member initiated a transfer to another person
